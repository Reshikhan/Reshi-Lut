LUT questions, answered

What is a LUT?

A LUT (Look-Up Table) is a file — usually .cube — that remaps every color in your video to create a film look, like teal-and-orange or warm vintage. Colorists use them in DaVinci Resolve and Premiere; here you apply them free in the browser.

Will my export keep the original quality?

Yes — the video is rendered at its native resolution (up to 4K) with high-bitrate encoding, so it stays visually identical to a pro export. Audio is preserved too.

Can I use my own LUTs?

Yes — tap Upload .cube LUT and use any 3D LUT file you own, including ones bought from creators.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally with WebGL in your browser — your footage never leaves your device.

What format does it export?

MP4 (H.264) where your browser supports it, otherwise WebM (VP9) — picked automatically for the best quality.
